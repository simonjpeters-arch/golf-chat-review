4. Inclusion criteria

Confirmed no-show

  1. Planned or booked golf together
  2. One party did not attend or both cancelled
  3. Identifiable date
  4. Clear attribution (Simon / Gaythan / Both)

Borderline

  • Ambiguous golf context
  • Double-booking awareness
  • Aggregate accusations without one dated round
  • Wrong activity (swim, virtual PGA, etc.)
  • Forgot to book

False positive

Looked like skip but they played (reluctance then rescheduled).

8. Limitations
